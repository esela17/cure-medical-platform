💸 Unit Economics & Revenue Model

Detailed financial mechanics of the CURE marketplace

💵 Cash Flow — Cash Payment (Primary)

The most common payment method in the Egyptian market:

  • Patient pays EGP 500 cash directly to nurse
  • System debits EGP 150 (30%) from nurse's cashBalance
  • Nurse owes platform EGP 150 → settled periodically
  • Credit limit: EGP -5,000 per nurse with auto-suspension at -4,500

💳 Cash Flow — Online Payment

Growing digital payment channel via Paymob:

  • Patient pays EGP 500 online via Paymob gateway
  • Platform receives EGP 500 directly
  • Platform credits EGP 350 (70%) to nurse's onlineBalance
  • Platform retains EGP 150 (30%) — clean cash flow
